Closing Argument · The Non-Obvious Insight Summary

They don't sell virality.
They sell fundraise-to-launch conversion.

The Official Proposition

“Algorithmic research + 300M-view network + filmmakers = guaranteed viral launch.”

What The Forensic Record Shows

Every client arrived pre-credible. Every launch was founder-voiced, timed on a weekday afternoon, and opened with money. The cinematic film represents the final 10% — selection, voice, timing, and capital narrative constitute the remaining 90%.

The guarantee is real — but it is an underwriting mechanism, not a distribution miracle. Like an insurer agreeing only to underwrite buildings that are already fireproof: “never failed once” is survivorship achieved by disciplined client filtering. The resulting asset simultaneously services hiring, enterprise sales, and investor signalling.

Falsifiable prediction: If they ever launch an unfunded, unknown, brand-voiced company on a quiet weekend morning — and it goes viral — this thesis breaks. Until then, the client filter is the formula.

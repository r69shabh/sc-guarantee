# THE GUARANTEE — Case File on Social Capital Inc.

An interactive investigative autopsy of Social Capital Inc.'s "guaranteed viral launch" claim, examined strictly through their own public record: 9 launches, 9 founder tweets, 7 films.

Designed as an **archival print-magazine case dossier** (inspired by *The Atlantic*, *The New Yorker*, and *The Economist*).

## Overview & Evidence

- **Exhibit A · The Money Plot**: 8 of 9 launches opened with an immense valuation, raise, or ARR figure.
- **Exhibit B · The Network**: 8 of 9 launches originated on the founder's personal account rather than brand channels.
- **Exhibit C · The Timeline**: Keynote temporal discipline — weekday afternoons, zero improvisation.
- **Exhibit D · The Hook Lab**: Five copywriting archetypes deconstructing the rhetorical formulas.
- **Exhibit E · Guarantee Calculator**: Base-rate probability calculator based on Social Capital's qualification criteria.
- **The Film Room**: Complete video vault and text-only confessionals.

## Running Locally

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or run a local static web server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
